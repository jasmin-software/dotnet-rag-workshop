# Sample Company Handbook

> **Sample only:** This handbook is a simple example for demonstration purposes.

## 1. Welcome & Company Basics

### 1.1 Welcome

Welcome to the company. We are glad to have you on the team and look forward to working together.

### 1.2 Our Workplace

We aim to maintain a respectful, collaborative, inclusive, and professional workplace.

### 1.3 Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to perform their duties responsibly, communicate clearly, and follow company policies.

### 1.4 Handbook Updates

The company may update this handbook as policies, benefits, or business needs change.

## 2. Employment & Workplace Conduct

### 2.1 Equal Opportunity

The company is committed to providing equal employment opportunities and a workplace free from unlawful discrimination.

### 2.2 Respectful Conduct

Employees must treat coworkers, customers, and business partners with professionalism and respect.

### 2.3 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company, customer, and employee information.

### 2.4 Conflicts of Interest

Employees should disclose situations in which personal interests could conflict with company responsibilities.

## 3. Work Hours & Remote Work

### 3.1 Regular Work Schedule

Employees are expected to work their assigned schedules and remain available during required working hours.

### 3.2 Work From Home

Work from home (WFH) is only allowed on Tuesday, unless an exception is approved by the company.

### 3.3 Remote Work Equipment Allowance

Employees may receive an allowance of up to **\$100** for approved remote work equipment.

### 3.4 Working Outside Canada

Employees may spend up to **10 calendar days** working outside Canada, subject to prior approval and any applicable legal, tax, security, or operational requirements.

## 4. Time Off & Leaves

### 4.1 Vacation

Employees earn **15 days of vacation per year**.

### 4.2 Paid Holidays

The company observes **11.5 paid holidays per calendar year**.

### 4.3 Sick Leave

Employees earn **1 day of sick leave per month of continuous service**.

### 4.4 Other Leaves

Employees may be eligible for other job-protected or company-approved leaves in accordance with applicable law and company policy.

## 5. Medical Insurance

### 5.1 Eligibility

Employees are eligible for medical insurance after **one month of employment**.

### 5.2 Coverage

The plan covers **standard medical services**, subject to the terms, limits, and exclusions of the applicable insurance plan.

### 5.3 Premium Contributions

The company contributes **80% of the medical insurance premium**. Employees are responsible for the remaining portion.

### 5.4 Enrollment & Changes

Eligible employees will receive enrollment information. Changes to coverage may be permitted when allowed by the plan or following an eligible life event.

## 6. Dental Insurance

### 6.1 Eligibility

Eligible employees may enroll in the company dental insurance plan according to the plan's enrollment rules.

### 6.2 Coverage

Dental coverage may include routine examinations, cleanings, and other eligible dental services, subject to plan terms.

### 6.3 Premiums

Employee and company premium contributions are determined by the applicable dental plan and communicated during enrollment.

### 6.4 Claims & Plan Details

Coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles, and claim procedures are governed by the official dental plan documents.

## 7. Compensation & Performance

### 7.1 Payroll

Employees are paid according to the company's regular payroll schedule.

### 7.2 Compensation Reviews

Compensation may be reviewed periodically based on role, performance, market conditions, and company circumstances.

### 7.3 Performance Feedback

Managers and employees are encouraged to discuss goals, progress, and development regularly.

### 7.4 Expenses

Reasonable and approved business expenses will be reimbursed according to company procedures.

## 8. Technology, Security & Departure

### 8.1 Acceptable Technology Use

Company systems and devices should be used responsibly, securely, and primarily for legitimate business purposes.

### 8.2 Information Security

Employees must use reasonable security practices, protect passwords, and promptly report suspected security incidents.

### 8.3 Company Property

Company property must be cared for appropriately and returned when requested or when employment ends.

### 8.4 Leaving the Company

Departing employees must complete required offboarding steps, return company property, and continue to protect confidential information after employment ends.
